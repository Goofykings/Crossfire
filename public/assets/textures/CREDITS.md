# Texture and environment credits

All external textures and HDRIs come from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain, no attribution required). Credit is listed anyway as a courtesy.

Files were downloaded by `tools/fetch_textures.py`, resized and re-encoded as JPG. Normal maps use the OpenGL (+Y) convention; `_orm` maps are Poly Haven's `arm` maps (R = AO, G = roughness, B = metallic).
